    a: "Many strong pre-workouts pack 200–350 mg of caffeine per serving. General guidance puts up to about 400 mg per day as safe for most healthy adults, but tolerance varies. If you're sensitive or new, start with a half scoop and avoid taking it within several hours of bedtime."

    a: "For most healthy adults, the well-studied ingredients are generally safe at label doses, with tingling (beta-alanine) and jitters (caffeine) being common.
