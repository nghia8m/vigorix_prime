faqs:
  - q: "Which form of magnesium is best?"
    a: "For most men, chelated forms like glycinate (bisglycinate) or malate offer the best balance of absorption and gut tolerance. Citrate is well absorbed but can loosen stools, while cheap oxide is poorly absorbed and mainly acts as a laxative. Match the form to your goal — glycinate for sleep and stress, malate for energy."
  - q: "How much magnesium should I take?"
    a: "Adult men need roughly 400–420 mg of total magnesium daily from food and supplements combined, so most supplement to fill a gap of 100–300 mg of elemental magnesium. Always read the elemental amount on the label, not the total compound weight, and don't overshoot."
  - q: "When should I take magnesium?"
    a: "Timing is flexible, but many men take glycinate in the evening because it may support relaxation and sleep. Taking it with food can improve tolerance. Consistency day to day matters more than the exact time."
  - q: "Is it safe to take magnesium every day?"
    a: "For healthy men, daily supplemental magnesium at typical doses is generally safe, with loose stools being the most common side effect if you take too much. This isn't medical advice — talk to your doctor first if you have kidney issues, take medication, or aren't sure how much you need."
