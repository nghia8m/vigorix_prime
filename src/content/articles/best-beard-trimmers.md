faqs:
  - q: "What length setting should I use for stubble?"
    a: "For a short designer-stubble look, most men land around 1mm to 3mm, while a fuller 'five o'clock shadow' sits closer to 0.5mm to 1mm. Trimmers with fine dial adjustment, like the Panasonic ER-GB96 or Braun BT7340, make it easy to find and repeat your exact setting."
  - q: "Are ceramic or steel blades better?"
    a: "Steel blades are proven, affordable and easy to sharpen or replace, which is why most trimmers use them. Ceramic blades, like the Brio Beardscape's, run cooler and hold their edge longer, but can chip if dropped. For most men either is fine; ceramic is a nice upgrade for frequent, detailed trimming."
  - q: "Can I use a beard trimmer to cut my hair?"
    a: "Some, like the Philips All-in-One 9000 and Panasonic ER-GB96, include longer combs and can handle light head-hair duty. But dedicated hair clippers have wider blades and taper better for fades. For occasional buzz cuts a versatile trimmer works; for regular haircuts, buy a proper clipper."
  - q: "How do I keep a trimmer's blades sharp?"
    a: "Brush out hair after each use, rinse washable models, and oil the blades periodically with a drop of clipper oil. Self-sharpening steel blades last for years with this care, and most manufacturers sell replacement blade heads when they eventually dull."
