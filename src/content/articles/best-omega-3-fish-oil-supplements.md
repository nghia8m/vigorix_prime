faqs:
  - q: "How much EPA and DHA do I actually need?"
    a: "Many general recommendations land around 250–500 mg of combined EPA and DHA per day for healthy adults, with higher amounts sometimes used for specific goals. Read the EPA/DHA line, not just the total 'fish oil' number, since that headline figure often overstates the active dose."
  - q: "Triglyceride vs ethyl ester — does form matter?"
    a: "The triglyceride form is generally absorbed more efficiently than the ethyl ester form, so it's a small point in favor of triglyceride oils. Ethyl ester products are usually cheaper and still effective when taken with a meal, so form is one factor among potency, purity and price."
  - q: "Why does third-party testing matter for fish oil?"
    a: "Fish oil can oxidize (go rancid) and may carry trace contaminants from the ocean. Third-party testing — from programs like IFOS, USP or NSF — verifies potency, freshness and purity, which is why it's worth prioritizing when choosing a brand."
  - q: "Are omega-3 supplements safe?"
    a: "For most healthy adults, fish oil is well tolerated, with mild fishy burps or digestive upset being the most common complaints. This isn't medical advice — talk to your doctor before starting if you take blood thinners, have a bleeding disorder, or have a fish or shellfish allergy."
