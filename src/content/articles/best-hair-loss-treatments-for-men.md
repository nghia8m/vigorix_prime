  - q: "What's the difference between finasteride and minoxidil?"
    a: "Finasteride is an oral or topical prescription that lowers DHT, the hormone driving male pattern loss, to slow shedding. Minoxidil is an over-the-counter topical that helps regrow and thicken hair but doesn't affect DHT. They work through different mechanisms, and using both together generally outperforms either one alone."
  - q: "Do hair supplements like Nutrafol actually work?"
    a: "Nutrafol has more clinical backing than most hair supplements and may help men whose shedding is tied to stress or nutrition. That said, its effects are modest and less certain than finasteride or minoxidil, and it shouldn't be expected to reverse significant pattern baldness on its own. Many men use it as a complement rather than a replacement."
  - q: "How long until I see results, and do I have to keep using treatment?"
    a: "Give any proven treatment at least 3–6 months, and up to a year, before judging results. Hair loss is progressive, so these treatments work by maintaining and regrowing hair over time — if you stop, gains typically reverse within months. This is general information, not medical advice; a clinician can tailor a plan to you."
