faqs:
  - q: "Can any product actually make my beard grow thicker?"
    a: "Your beard's density and coverage are mostly set by genetics, age and hormones. Minoxidil has the strongest evidence for stimulating hair growth and micro-needling has a plausible mechanism, but neither guarantees results and both take months of consistent use. Oils and most serums improve how a beard looks and feels rather than growing new follicles."
  - q: "Is it safe to use Rogaine (minoxidil) on my beard?"
    a: "Using minoxidil on the beard is an off-label application — it's FDA-approved for the scalp, not the face — and it's commonly discussed in grooming communities. Some men see results, but it can cause dryness or irritation, gains often reverse if you stop, and it's wise to speak with a dermatologist before starting, especially if you have sensitive skin."
  - q: "How long until I see results?"
    a: "For growth-oriented approaches like micro-needling or minoxidil, plan on at least 8–16 weeks of consistent use before judging anything, and understand results vary widely. Conditioning oils improve appearance almost immediately but don't change actual growth."
  - q: "Do beard oils help the beard grow?"
    a: "Not directly. Beard oils condition the hair and the skin beneath it, reducing itch and flyaways so the beard looks fuller and healthier. That improved appearance is valuable, but oils don't create new follicles or meaningfully speed up growth."
