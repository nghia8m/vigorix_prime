faqs:
  - q: "How much protein do I actually need per day?"
    a: "Most active men benefit from roughly 0.7–1g of protein per pound of bodyweight per day to support muscle growth and recovery. Whey makes it easy to close the gap, but aim to get most of your protein from whole foods and use one or two scoops to top up."
  - q: "Whey concentrate, isolate or hydrolysate — what's the difference?"
    a: "Concentrate is the least processed, creamiest and cheapest, but contains more lactose, fat and carbs. Isolate is filtered to be leaner and lower in lactose. Hydrolysate is partially pre-digested for the fastest absorption. For most men a quality concentrate or blend is fine; choose isolate or hydrolysate if you're lactose-sensitive."
  - q: "When is the best time to take whey protein?"
    a: "Total daily protein matters far more than precise timing. A shake within an hour or two of training is convenient for recovery, but any time that helps you hit your daily target works — first thing in the morning, between meals, or before bed."
  - q: "Is whey protein safe to take every day?"
    a: "For healthy men, whey is safe and well tolerated as a daily supplement. If you have a dairy allergy or lactose intolerance, choose an isolate or a non-dairy option, and check with your doctor if you have kidney issues or another medical condition."
