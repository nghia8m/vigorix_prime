  - q: "Do I need to load creatine?"

  - q: "Is creatine monohydrate better than other forms?"
    a: "Yes, for value and evidence. Monohydrate is by far the most studied form and works as well as or better than pricier 'advanced' versions like HCl or buffered creatine. There's no proven benefit to paying more, so plain monohydrate remains the gold standard."
  - q: "Will creatine make me hold water or gain fat?"
    a: "Creatine draws a small amount of water into your muscle cells, which can add a pound or two on the scale early on, but this is intramuscular, not bloat, and it does not cause fat gain. Any lasting weight change comes from the muscle and training it supports."
  - q: "Is creatine safe to take long term?"
    a: "Creatine is one of the safest and most researched supplements available, with studies supporting daily use over years in healthy adults. If you have a kidney condition or take medication, check with your doctor first, and stay well hydrated."
