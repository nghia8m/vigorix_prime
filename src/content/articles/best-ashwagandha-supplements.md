  - q: "KSM-66 vs Sensoril — which is better?"
    a: "Both are branded, standardized extracts with solid research. KSM-66 is a root-only extract often used for testosterone, strength and general support, while Sensoril is a root-and-leaf extract standardized to higher withanolides and leans toward stress and sleep. For most men's goals, KSM-66 at 600 mg is the well-trodden choice."
  - q: "How much ashwagandha should I take?"
    a: "Most clinical studies use 300–600 mg of a standardized extract per day, commonly 600 mg of KSM-66. Higher isn't automatically better, and generic root powder needs much larger, less reliable amounts. Give it several weeks of consistent daily use to judge results."
  - q: "Is ashwagandha safe to take daily?"
    a: "For most healthy men it's well tolerated at standard doses, though some cycle it after a few months. This isn't medical advice — talk to your doctor before starting, especially if you have a thyroid or autoimmune condition, take sedatives or thyroid medication, or have liver concerns."
