  - q: "Do greens powders actually replace vegetables?"
    a: "No. Greens powders are a supplement, not a replacement for whole produce, which provides fiber, water and a fuller spectrum of nutrients that processing strips away. Think of a greens powder as insurance for the days your vegetable intake falls short, not as your main source of them."
  - q: "What should I look for in a greens powder?"
    a: "Favor products that disclose ingredient doses rather than hiding them in a proprietary blend, carry third-party testing, and avoid excess added sugar. Useful extras include probiotics, adaptogens and a real dose of greens like spirulina or chlorella, but transparency and dosing matter more than a long ingredient count."
  - q: "When is the best time to take a greens powder?"
    a: "There's no critical timing — consistency is what matters. Many men take it first thing in the morning mixed with water, but any time you'll remember it daily works. If it contains caffeine or energizing adaptogens, earlier in the day is usually better for sleep."
  - q: "Are greens powders safe to take every day?"
    a: "For healthy men, quality greens powders are generally safe for daily use. Because some contain herbs, adaptogens or high vitamin K from greens, check with your doctor if you take medication such as blood thinners, have a medical condition, or are unsure about an ingredient."
