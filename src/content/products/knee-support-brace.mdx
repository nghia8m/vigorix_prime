## What this is

A sleeve that pulls on over the knee, with two adjustable straps on each side so
you can set how snug it sits. The panel over the kneecap is open rather than
solid, which keeps the front of the joint free to move while the sleeve stays in
place.

It is intended as everyday wear — around the house, on a walk, at a desk — rather
than as clinical equipment.

## How it feels

The fabric is light and stretchy, with a textured inner face so the sleeve does
not slide down as you move. Straps let you go from barely-there to firmly snug;
most people settle somewhere in the middle after a day or two of adjusting.

It is warm. Under trousers on a hot day you will notice it. Individual results
vary, and comfort depends heavily on getting the size right.

## Choosing a size

Measure around the middle of your kneecap with the leg straight, then check that
figure against the size guide supplied in the pack. If you fall between two
sizes, the larger one is usually the more comfortable choice for long wear.

## How to use

1. Pull the sleeve up over the foot and into position with the open panel centred on the kneecap.
2. Fasten the straps one at a time, working from the bottom up, until it is snug but not tight.
3. Loosen it if you feel pins and needles, a throbbing pulse, or any change in skin colour below the knee.
4. Take it off before sleeping.

Stop using it and speak to a qualified healthcare professional if you have a
diagnosed knee condition, a recent injury, circulation problems, or if wearing it
makes anything feel worse.

## Questions

### Can I wear it while exercising?

It is built for everyday wear rather than for sport. If you want support during
training, talk to a physiotherapist about what suits your activity.

### Will it fit either knee?

Yes, the sleeve is symmetrical and fits the left or right knee.

### Is this a medical device?

No. It is a general wellness product, not a medicine and not a medical device,
and we make no medical claims for it. Anyone with an existing knee problem should
speak to a qualified healthcare professional before using it.
