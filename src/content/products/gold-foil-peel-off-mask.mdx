## What this is

A cosmetic peel-off mask with a gold tint. It goes on as a smooth layer, sets as
it dries, then lifts away in one piece rather than being rinsed off.

## How to use

1. Start with clean, dry skin.
2. Spread an even layer, thick enough to hold together — a thin patch tears when you lift it.
3. Avoid the eye area, eyebrows, lips and the hairline.
4. Leave it until it is dry to the touch, then lift from the edge and peel upwards in one piece.

Patch-test on a small area first and leave it a day before using it on the whole
face. Stop using it if your skin stings, reddens or feels sore, and do not apply
it to broken, irritated or sunburnt skin. Individual results vary.
