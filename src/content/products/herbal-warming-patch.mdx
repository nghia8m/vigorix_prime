## What this is

A thin, self-adhesive patch with a mugwort-based herbal blend held against the
skin by a fabric backing. You peel it off its liner, place it where you want it,
and it stays put under clothing until you take it off. Each patch is single use.

The material is flexible enough to follow the shape of a knee, shoulder or lower
back without bunching, and the profile is low enough that it is not obvious under
a jumper or a pair of jeans.

## How it feels

Most people notice a gradual warming sensation building over the first several
minutes rather than an immediate change. The warmth is mild and steady, not hot.
The herbal blend has a distinct, slightly bitter scent that fades once the patch
is covered by clothing.

Individual results vary, and how noticeable the warmth feels differs from person
to person and from one part of the body to another.

## How to use

1. Start with clean, dry skin. Skip any area with broken skin, a rash or a recent injury.
2. Peel the patch away from its backing liner and press it into place, smoothing the edges down.
3. Wear it for the time stated on the pack, then peel it away slowly and discard it. Do not reuse a patch.
4. If the skin under a patch becomes uncomfortable at any point, take it off.

Do not use patches on children, during pregnancy, or alongside any other
skin-applied product on the same area unless a healthcare professional has told
you it is fine to do so.

## Questions

### How many patches should I start with?

The 8-patch pack is the usual starting point if you have not tried this style of
patch before. The larger packs cost less per patch and suit people who already
know they get on with them.

### Can I wear one under clothing at work?

Yes. The patch is thin and matt, and it is designed to sit flat under normal
clothing. The herbal scent is noticeable when the patch is uncovered.

### Is this a medicine?

No. It is a general wellness product, not a medicine and not a medical device,
and we make no medical claims for it. If you have a specific health concern, or
you are already under medical care, talk to a qualified healthcare professional
before using it.
