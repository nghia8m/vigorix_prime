## What this is

A flat pad that starts warming as soon as you open the wrapper — no cable, no
batteries and nothing to heat up first. An adhesive strip on the back holds it
against the inside of a layer of clothing, so it travels with you.

Each pad is single use. Once it has cooled, it goes in the bin.

## How it feels

The warmth builds gradually after the wrapper comes off, then holds steady for
several hours before fading. It is designed to be a low, even warmth you stop
noticing, not a hot spot. Individual results vary — how warm it feels depends on
how many layers sit between the pad and your skin.

## How to use

1. Open the wrapper and take out the pad. It starts warming on contact with air.
2. Peel the adhesive strip and stick the pad to the **outside of a layer of clothing** — never directly onto skin.
3. Leave it in place while you need it, then peel it off and discard it once it has cooled.

Do not use a pad on skin, under a tight strap, while sleeping, or on anyone who
cannot easily move away from a heat source or tell you they are too warm. If you
have reduced skin sensation, circulation problems or diabetes, check with a
qualified healthcare professional first.
