## What this is

A lightweight V-shape massager that sits across the jaw and cheeks, held by a
strap that loops over the ears. Gel pads make the contact with the skin, and the
intensity is adjustable.

## How it feels

A light tingling or tapping sensation across the jaw and cheeks that gets
stronger as you raise the intensity. Start at the lowest setting. Individual
results vary, and this is a cosmetic massager — it is not a weight-loss device
and does not change the shape of your face.

## How to use

1. Fit fresh gel pads to the contact points and clip the strap over your ears.
2. Start at the lowest intensity and only raise it if it stays comfortable.
3. Keep to the session length the instructions give, then remove it and clean the pads.

Do not use it if you have a pacemaker or any other implanted electronic device,
and do not use it on broken or irritated skin, over the eyes, or on the front of
the neck. If you are pregnant, have a heart condition or epilepsy, speak to a
qualified healthcare professional before using it.
