## How to use

1. Start with clean, dry skin.
2. Open a sachet, unfold the sheet and lay it flat against the face, lining up the eye and mouth openings.
3. Leave it on for the time the instructions give, then lift it off and discard it.
4. Pat any remaining serum into the skin. There is nothing to rinse.

Patch-test on a small area first and leave it a day before using it on the whole
face. Keep it out of the eyes, stop using it if your skin stings or reddens, and
do not apply it to broken, irritated or sunburnt skin. Individual results vary.

## Questions

### Does it shrink pores?

No, and no cosmetic product can. Pore size is largely fixed. A hydrating mask
can leave skin feeling smoother and looking more even while the effect lasts,
which is the honest version of that claim.
