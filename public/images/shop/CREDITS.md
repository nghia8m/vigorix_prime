# Shop image credits

**These are stand-in photographs, not photographs of the products being sold.**
They were pulled from Wikimedia Commons so the shop can be laid out, reviewed
and demonstrated with something better than a grey box. Every one of them shows
*a* knee brace, *a* sheet mask, *a* heat pad — not the item in the supplier's
catalogue.

Replace all of them with the supplier's own photography before this shop takes
a real order. Until then every product is `draft: true`, and the production
build refuses to run.

its own terms on Commons — mostly CC BY-SA or public domain. **Check the licence
